Privacy

Your info, in plain words.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What I collect

Only what you type and send me: your name, your business or project, your Instagram, your phone number, your message, and the answers to the three optional questions after Send.

If you text, call, email or message me on Instagram instead, I see what you send there, like your number or email.

Why

To call or text you back about what you asked for. That's it. No newsletters, no ads, no lists.

Who else touches it

  • Web3Forms carries the form to my email.
  • Google runs my email, so your message sits in my inbox. The site's fonts also load from Google, which sees your IP address when the page opens.
  • GitHub hosts this site. Like any host, its servers log basic visit info, such as your IP address.

I don't sell your info, and I don't share it with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. There is no analytics and no tracking, and I don't let other companies track you here.

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. Since I don't track you, the site works the same either way.

How long I keep it

As long as we're talking or working together. Ask me to delete it any time, and I will.
